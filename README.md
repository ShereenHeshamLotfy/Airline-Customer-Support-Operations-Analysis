# 🎧 Airline Customer Support Analysis – Operations & Efficiency Insights

This project analyzes **10,000+ airline customer support tickets** to evaluate **service efficiency, resolution time, and operational cost drivers**.

The goal is to identify bottlenecks in customer support and provide insights to improve **customer satisfaction and operational performance**.

---

## 💼 Key Highlights

- Analyzed **10K+ support tickets** across multiple countries and channels  
- Evaluated **resolution time (SLA), ticket priority, and cost drivers**  
- Identified bottlenecks affecting service efficiency  
- Generated actionable insights to improve support operations  

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The project focuses on analyzing customer support operations by:

- Measuring **resolution time and service efficiency**  
- Identifying **high-cost and high-frequency issues**  
- Analyzing **customer communication channels**  
- Evaluating **department workload and performance**  

---

## 👩‍💻 My Role

- Cleaned and prepared ticket data using Excel  
- Created calculated columns (e.g., resolution duration)  
- Built dashboards using Pivot Tables  
- Analyzed ticket lifecycle and operational metrics  
- Generated business insights and recommendations  

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- Average resolution time is **21 days**, indicating potential inefficiencies  
- **75% of tickets remain open**, highlighting backlog issues  
- High-priority tickets are **not resolved faster** than others  
- Top issue types (**Billing & Refunds ~30%**) drive most ticket volume  
- Certain countries (e.g., **Jordan, Lebanon**) generate the highest number of tickets  

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize high-priority tickets to reduce customer impact  
- Focus on top recurring issues to reduce ticket volume  
- Improve response time across all priority levels  
- Allocate resources to high-load departments  
- Optimize support channels (Chat & Email dominate usage)  

---

## 💰 Business Impact

- Reducing resolution time improves **customer satisfaction and retention**  
- Addressing high-volume issues reduces **operational workload**  
- Optimizing support channels improves **response efficiency**  
- Better resource allocation increases **team productivity**
